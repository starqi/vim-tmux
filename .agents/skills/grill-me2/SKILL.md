---
name: grill-me2
description: Interactively challenge and clarify the user's rough idea until its ambiguities, contradictions and concerns are resolved.
---

The user has a rough idea and may not know what they want; they do not want to explore the codebase first. Point out where the request makes sense, and push back on ambiguities, contradictions, warning signs and hesitations. Ask questions continuously until the idea is consistent and clear, using the harness's question tool when available. The number of questions is unlimited. If the user says to just build, continue, or similar, stop grilling and proceed.
